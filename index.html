FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything You Need to Know

Do I have to buy expensive proprietary hardware from you?
No. The Waiter is 100% open-hardware. You can run it on any iPad, Samsung tablet, cheap £80 Android tablet, iPhone, or Smart TV screen you already have. There are zero equipment fees and zero hardware lock-in.
How do table QR codes work for diners?
When a guest sits at Table 4 and scans the QR code with their phone camera, the menu opens instantly in their browser with Table 4 automatically pre-selected. They do not need to download an app. They select items, customize spices/sides, and pay. The ticket appears instantly on your Kitchen KDS.
Are there any hidden transaction fees or commissions?
Zero. Unlike Sunday or me&u, we charge 0% commission on your food and drink sales. You pay our flat monthly subscription (£59 to £89) and keep 100% of your takings via your own Stripe or card merchant account.
Is The Waiter compliant with UK hospitality statutory regulations?
Yes, fully compliant. It includes Natasha's Law (Food Information Regulations 2021) 14-allergen matrices, Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2024 Tronc tracking, statutory EHO digital fridge temperature logs, and Challenge 25 age verification warnings.
Where is the database stored and how can I host it?
The core system runs on a high-performance, single-file SQLite database (waiter.db) with 17 relational tables. You can self-host it easily on a Hostinger VPS with CloudPanel, Docker, or Render for under £5/month, or run it entirely on our cloud.
What happens when my 14-day free trial ends?
No credit card is required to begin. At the end of your 14-day trial, you can choose to activate any of our plans or export your data. There are no automatic billing traps or cancellation penalties.
