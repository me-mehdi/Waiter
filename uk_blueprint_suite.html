Ingredients: 28-day aged British beef sirloin, beef dripping roast potatoes, giant Yorkshire pudding, heritage carrots, bone marrow red wine gravy.

Plating Guide: Carved beef laid atop carrot puree, 3 roast potatoes crispy side up, Yorkshire pudding crowning dish, gravy poured tableside.
